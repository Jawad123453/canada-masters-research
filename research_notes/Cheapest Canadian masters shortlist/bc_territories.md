## RAW NOTES 1 — UNBC (University of Northern British Columbia)

Cost (2026/27 fee year, per UNBC finance "Tuition and fees" https://www.unbc.ca/finance/accounts-receivable/tuition-and-fees and Graduate Calendar "Fees" https://www.unbc.ca/calendar/graduate/fees — figures as returned by domain-restricted search; PARAPHRASE of table rows, not opened):

- MEng Integrated Wood Design: "$745.76" per credit hour international (2026/27); 2025-26 calendar: $731.14/credit.  Program = 8 courses + final project, 30 credits (https://www.unbc.ca/engineering/meng-integrated-wood-design, paraphrase). Minimum fee = three tuition fee units (paraphrase).
- MBA (MBA for Sustainable Leadership): $9,730.03/semester intl + $488.18 program fee/semester (paraphrase).
- Medical insurance fee $190.00/semester, mandatory, cannot be waived for grad students (paraphrase); International Student Fee $125/semester (paraphrase, Graduate Calendar Fees).
- Cost arithmetic MEng IWD (illustrative, 2026/27 rates): 30 cr x $745.76 = $22,372.80; + ~3-4 semesters x ($190 med + $125 intl fee) = $945-$1,260; + student-society/ancillary fees (not confirmed, PG campus only) => ~$23,300-$23,600 + ancillary + MSP (once MSP-eligible). Likely UNDER $30k but MSP and student fees UNCONFIRMED. Standard thesis/course master's at $2,704.15 x 6 semesters = $16,224.90 tuition — cheapest in BC.
