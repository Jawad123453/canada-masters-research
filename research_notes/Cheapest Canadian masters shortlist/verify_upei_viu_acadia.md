## A. UPEI Master of Arts in Island Studies (MAIS), course-based option

### Takeaway
MAIS is still the strongest Atlantic candidate. It states a percentage bar of "B, i.e. 70% to 79.9%" over the last two years, and it publishes a **program-specific per-course international fee ($972)**. On that basis the 2026-27 cost is about **CAD 28.1k**, which is under 30k but above the 27k fee-increase line. Fall and Winter intakes are stated.

Two items still decide the outcome:
- **Grade averaging.** No rule was found on how UPEI averages repeated or failed courses for applicants. The applicant is about 73% if retakes replace failures, and about 66-67% if all attempts count.
- **Fee basis.** UPEI also publishes a general "$9,718 per year" full-time graduate international fee. Its interaction with the MAIS per-course line is not confirmed in writing.

### Cited Findings
**Bar (F1)**
- "ordinarily be expected to have achieved an average of at least second class ("B", i.e. 70% to 79.9%) standing in the work of the last four semesters or the last two undergraduate years" (search excerpt). This is the Faculty of Arts minimum quoted on the MAIS page. Degree basis: "Bachelor's degree, or equivalent, in an honours or majors program" (search excerpt). — [UPEI MAIS program page](https://www.upei.ca/programs/island-studies); [UPEI Calendar MAIS](https://calendar.upei.ca/current/chapter/master-of-arts-ma-island-studies/)
- Repeated/failed courses in the **admission** average: no UPEI graduate rule found. Graduate regulations cover only in-program fails: "<60% ... deemed to have failed". MA candidates "must maintain a cumulative average grade of at least 75% in the substantive courses" (search excerpt). That 75% is an **in-program progression risk**, not an admission rule. — [UPEI Graduate Program Regulations](https://calendar.upei.ca/current/chapter/program-regulations-graduate-studies/)
- UPEI's undergraduate-oriented SAP policy: all attempts recorded, "highest grade calculated into the GPA" (search excerpt). It is not a graduate admission rule and should not be relied on. — [UPEI SAP policy PDF](https://files.upei.ca/studentaffairs/upei_satisfactory_academic_progress_sap_policy.pdf)
- WES:
  - No UPEI-wide graduate WES requirement was found. A WES course-by-course evaluation is required only on the DVM page. — [UPEI Calendar DVM](https://calendar.upei.ca/current/chapter/doctor-of-veterinary-medicine/)
  - The graduate admissions chapter asks applicants from outside North America for official statements of grades and subject matter. Other programs (MEd) ask for a notarized translation (paraphrase). — [UPEI Graduate Program Admissions](https://calendar.upei.ca/current/chapter/graduate-program-admissions/)
  - UPEI "will not pre-evaluate" transcripts (paraphrase). — [UPEI Graduate Admissions](https://www.upei.ca/admission-requirements/graduate-admissions)
- Lebanon equivalency for graduate admission: none found in this round. The country page entries found earlier were undergraduate. — [UPEI country-specific requirements](https://www.upei.ca/admission-requirements/country-specific-requirements)

**Fees, 2026-27 (F3)**
- MAIS fee lines:
  - "Master of Arts in Island Studies Tuition - $15,360 per program (Course-based study - $1,536 per course for 10 courses)" (search excerpt).
  - "International Fee (Master of Arts in Island Studies) - $972 per course" (search excerpt).
  - Source: [UPEI Tuition and Fees](https://www.upei.ca/fees?field_faculty_department_tid=All&field_level_value=All&field_time_value=All&field_type_value=All) and [Calendar Tuition and Fees](https://calendar.upei.ca/current/chapter/tuition-and-fees-2/).
- Both pages state the figures are "approved by the University of Prince Edward Island Board of Governors for the 2026–2027 academic year" (search excerpt).
- Conflicting general line: "For full-time graduate programs, the international fee is $9,718 per year" (search-tool summary of the fees page, not a verbatim line). The calendar's general "$972 per course" line is labelled "(part-time)". — same fees pages
- Inference: the MAIS-specific line ("International Fee (Master of Arts in Island Studies) - $972 per course") is a dedicated program line, parallel to the MEd line "International Fee (Master Of Education) - $972 Per Course". That makes the per-course basis the more likely one. It is still **UNCONFIRMED in writing**.
  - Note: 10 × 972 = 9,720 ≈ one year of 9,718. The two bases differ only if the program spans 2 billing years at 9,718 each (2 × 9,718 = 19,436).
- Health and dental:
  - "International Student Health Insurance - $1,000 may partially opt out with proof of insurance" (search excerpt).
  - "International Student Dental Insurance - $186" (search excerpt).
  - Together $1,186/yr. — [UPEI Tuition and Fees](https://www.upei.ca/fees?field_faculty_department_tid=All&field_level_value=All&field_time_value=All&field_type_value=All)
- Student fees: "Student Union Fee - $198", CFS $12, CASA $6, WUSC $10 (search excerpt). No graduate-association line was surfaced. — same page
- 2026-27 increases: tuition +6.5%, international fees +7.5%. — [UPEI budget news, Apr 2026](https://www.upei.ca/communications/news/2026/04/upei-board-governors-approves-balanced-2026-2027-operating-budget)
- Older handbook (≈2 years old): $1,442/course plus $904 international/course, and a "maintenance of status fee" of $500/term for extensions. — [MAIS handbook](https://files.upei.ca/arts/mais/mais_student_handbook.pdf)
- International deposit for course-based graduate programs: "$3,000 ... non-refundable and credited to tuition" (search excerpt). — [Calendar Tuition and Fees](https://calendar.upei.ca/current/chapter/tuition-and-fees-2/)

  - Sources: [UPEI MAIS](https://www.upei.ca/programs/island-studies); [MAIS scholarships](https://www.upei.ca/programs/island-studies/scholarships)

**Supervisor and prerequisites (F4/F6)**

The application asks for a statement of research interests and a writing sample. — [UPEI MAIS](https://www.upei.ca/programs/island-studies)

**Cost arithmetic (2026-27 basis)**
| Item | CAD |
|---|---|
| Tuition 10 × 1,536 | 15,360 |
| International fee 10 × 972 | 9,720 |
| Health + dental, 2 yrs × 1,186 | 2,372 |
| Student fees, 2 yrs × ~340 (SU 198 + CFS 12 + CASA 6 + WUSC 10 + others found earlier) | ~680 |
| **Total (per-course basis)** | **≈ 28,132** |
| Projected 2027-28 (+6.5% tuition, +7.5% intl): 10 × (1,636 + 1,045) + 2,372 + 680 | ≈ 29,862 |
| If the general 9,718/yr applies for 2 years instead: 15,360 + 19,436 + 2,372 + 680 | ≈ 37,848 → FAIL |

- 2026-27 total ≈ 28.1k, so it is in the fee-increase-risk zone (≥27k).
- On a 2027-28 start, the total is near 29.9k, which is razor-thin.
- Any extra term (maintenance fee or health) pushes it past 30k.
