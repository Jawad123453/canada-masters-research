## A. University of Guelph: Master of Capacity Development & Extension (course-based / MSc CDE coursework option)

### Takeaway
- **Cost:** About CAD 27.9k for 3 semesters and about CAD 29.2k–29.8k for 4. Both are under 30k, but both are over 27k, so both carry fee-increase risk.
  - New finding: CDE is "billed per credit", and the $24,141 (2027-28) is **full-program tuition**, not a per-year charge (search excerpts).
  - So a 4th semester adds only compulsory fees and UHIP, not another third of the tuition.
- **Filter 1 has two blockers:**
  - Guelph explicitly equates its 70% (B-) bar with **"3.0 on the US 4.0 scale"** (search excerpt). The applicant's WES GPAs (2.68 cumulative, ~2.90 last two years) are below that.
  - The only Lebanon threshold found is still **"80% or 15/20"**. It sits in a PDF of unknown date. The live guideline page (Lebanon entry "effective 2025-10-01") did not show its threshold in any excerpt.
- **Verdict:** UNCONFIRMED, leaning FAIL on Filter 1.

### Inferences
- **Cost arithmetic** (2027-28 full-program tuition; Fall 2026 fees as a proxy):
  - 3 semesters: 24,141 + 3 × 937.21 (2,811.63) + 948 = **27,900.63**.
  - 4 semesters (4th = Fall 2028): 24,141 + 4 × 937.21 (3,748.84) + 948 + a second-year UHIP of 948 = **29,785.84**. If the second-year UHIP is pro-rated at about 316 for 4 months: **29,153.84**.
  - 6 semesters (if "24 months" is billed at fees-per-semester only): 24,141 + 6 × 937.21 (5,623.26) + 2 × 948 = **31,660.26**, which FAILS.
  - **Verdict:** PASS at 3–4 semesters, with fee-increase risk (≥27k). Compulsory fees for 2027-28 and 2028-29 will rise, so 4 semesters could cross 30k.
  - The $2,000 tuition deposit for international course-based students is credited toward tuition, so it is not an extra cost.
- **Filter 1 is the likely failure point:**
  1. If the old Lebanon row (80% / 15/20) is still in force, 14.1/20 fails.
  2. If Guelph converts through a US-style GPA, its own mapping of 70% to 3.0 means WES 2.68 (and the ~2.90 last-two-years figure) fails.
  3. The pass case needs the in-house assessment to treat a ~73% last-two-years Lebanese average (retakes replacing failures) as ≥70%. The available evidence makes that unlikely.
- Repeated-course rule: not found on any Guelph page.
- **Honours:** "or the equivalent". The current guideline lists a 4-year Lebanese Licence as the master's minimum (paraphrase), so a 4-year (here 5-year) BME likely counts. Not explicitly confirmed.
- **Filters 4, 5 and 6:** PASS. No supervisor (the Graduate Program Coordinator advises); on campus; any discipline accepted.
- **PGWP length:** three or more semesters is more than 8 months, so it PASSES.
