### Cited Findings

The exceptions are English, History, Mathematics & Statistics and the MPA, which require 75% — [Admission Standards](https://www.uregina.ca/graduate-studies-research/graduate-calendar/admission-standards.html); [2026-2027 Graduate Calendar PDF](https://www.uregina.ca/graduate-studies-research/assets/docs/fgsr-docs/2026-2027-graduate-calendar.pdf)

The same page has a stray "University of Guelph" reference, which looks like a copy-paste error, so treat the page as imperfectly edited — [FGSR Eligibility Requirements](https://www.uregina.ca/graduate-studies-research/future-students/eligibility-requirements.html)

The M.Eng. is not exempted — [Graduate Studies Application Guide](https://www.uregina.ca/graduate-studies-research/future-students/how-to-apply.html); [How to Submit Documents](https://www.uregina.ca/graduate-studies-research/future-students/how-to-submit-documents.html); [FGSR Eligibility Requirements](https://www.uregina.ca/graduate-studies-research/future-students/eligibility-requirements.html)

### Inferences
- **Cost arithmetic for a 30-credit-hour M.Eng.** Assumes 6 credit hours per term over 5 terms (about 20 months); 9 credit hours per term would mean about 4 terms. Fall 2027 entrants pay 2027-28 rates, which are not published.
  - **Scenario A** (the international column is the all-in rate):
    - Tuition: $3,881 / 6 = $646.83 per credit hour (2025-26) × 1.03 = $666.24 (2026-27) × 30 = **$19,987**
    - Mandatory fees: $309 × 1.03 × 5 = **$1,591**
    - Health/dental: $225.85 × 2 = **$452**
    - **Total ≈ $22,030 (2026-27 basis)**; with another +3% for 2027-28, about $22,660. **PASS.**
  - **Scenario B** (separate international surcharge of about $1,334.50 per term; that figure is from the Levene page and undated): $22,030 + 5 × $1,334.50 = **≈ $28,700**. Under $30k, but a **fee-increase risk**.
  - **FGSR "$16,000/yr" cross-check:** $16,000 × 1.67 years ≈ $26,700, plus about $2,000 in fees and health ≈ $28,700. This matches Scenario B. If the program takes a full 2 years: $32,000 + fees → **FAIL**. Finishing in 4 terms at 9 credit hours per term protects the budget.
- The international-domestic gap in the 202610 table is $3,881 − $2,128 = $1,753 for 6 credit hours, which suggests the international column already includes the surcharge (Scenario A). This rests on a garbled table reading, so it stays UNCONFIRMED.
- **WES is mandatory for M.Eng.**, so admissions is very likely to use the WES ICAP cumulative GPA (2.68). That is good for the applicant, because WES excludes failed attempts later passed. The margin is 0.01.
